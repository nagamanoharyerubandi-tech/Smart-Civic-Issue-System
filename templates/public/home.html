How The Resolution Process Works

A structured, auditable 4-step pipeline that guarantees citizen voices turn into tangible public repairs.

1. Snap & Submit

Citizen snaps a photo of the problem, provides location, and gets a unique Reference ID.

2. Triage & Assign

Admins review complaints, run duplicate checks, set priority, and assign to specific department heads.

3. Field Action

Department maintenance crews are dispatched with machinery to execute field repairs.

4. Resolve & Rate

Issue is verified resolved with notes. The citizen receives SMS/Email notification and rates the work.
