Step-by-Step Guide

How The System Works

From discovering a pothole to verifying its repair — here is how your complaint moves through our municipal resolution pipeline.

1

Citizen Reports Issue

Take a photo using your smartphone or upload an image file. Choose the relevant category (e.g. Road Damage, Water Leakage, Garbage Accumulation) and provide your ward/area details. You can even use your phone's GPS for exact pinpointing.

Output: System generates a unique complaint reference code (e.g. CIV-202611-AB12) and sends an instant acknowledgment email & in-app alert.
2

Triage & Duplicate Check

The central municipal control room reviews the incoming complaint. Our built-in duplicate detection engine scans active complaints in the same ward to ensure duplicate reports are identified and linked.

Action: Priority is classified (Low, Medium, or High) based on safety hazard severity.
3

Department Assignment & Field Dispatch

The complaint is formally assigned to the responsible engineering department (such as PWD, Solid Waste Management, or Water Supply Board). Specific dispatch instructions and target completion dates are set.

Status: Complaint transitions to Assigned and then In Progress as maintenance teams deploy on-site.
4

Resolution & Citizen Feedback

Once field work is concluded, engineers log the resolution notes into the system. The complaint is marked Resolved, and the citizen receives a notification prompting them to rate the quality of service (1 to 5 stars).

Accountability: Average ratings are displayed on public and admin dashboards to measure civic performance.
